Privacy policy

ZadCollect is used only by its owner, Mohamed Shadi (imohamedshadi@gmail.com).

The app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. It can only see and change files that it creates in the owner's Drive. It does not read other files, and it does not share or sell any data.

No data from other users is collected. To remove access, revoke the app at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
